Terrors to Unveil: Last Trace

The third chapter of the Terrors to Unveil series. Follow a new story filled with mystery, fear and unexpected discoveries.

About This Game

Jack Williams, a 26 year old hard-working man, is finally taking a day off after a long week at work. He decides to spend his day off at his quiet cottage for a weekend of relaxation. Little did he know what would happen during his stay there.
