ABOUT THE CREATOR

PsychoVerse

My name is Dionisis Papadimitropoulos, an independent game developer from Greece. I am the creator behind PsychoVerse, a studio focused on creating atmospheric horror experiences, immersive worlds and memorable stories. I graduated from AKTO College with a degree in Game Design, where I developed my passion for game development, storytelling and creating unique interactive experiences. Through PsychoVerse, my goal is to create horror games that leave a lasting impression on players.
